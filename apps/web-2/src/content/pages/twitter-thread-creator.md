# X (Twitter) Threads Creator

Write, reorder, and preview your thread in one place. Free, no sign-up.

Draft, reorder, and ship X (Twitter) threads in a clean, distraction-free workspace.

## How to use it

Open https://www.usenotra.com/twitter-thread-creator in a browser. The builder starts with three example posts that explain it:

1. Welcome to the X thread builder. Type into any post to edit it. Press ⌘/Ctrl + Enter or hit Add post to chain another one underneath.
2. Got the order wrong? Hover a post and drag the handle on the right to move it. Click the X to delete a post you don't want.
3. Make it yours: click the avatar to upload a photo, and click the name or @handle to customize the author. Replace these three posts and you're off.

- Type into any post to edit it.
- Press Cmd + Enter (Mac) or Ctrl + Enter while an input is focused, or click Add post, to add a post underneath.
- Drag a post's handle to reorder it. Click the X to delete it.
- Click the avatar to upload a photo, and click the name or @handle to set the author shown in the preview.

## Limits

- 280 characters per post, with a live counter.
- Display name up to 50 characters, handle up to 15.
- Everything stays in your browser. Nothing is posted to X for you.
