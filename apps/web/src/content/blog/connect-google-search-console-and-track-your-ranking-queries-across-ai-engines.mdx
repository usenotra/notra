Connect your Search Console account and pick a property, and Notra syncs your ranking queries every week. Those queries show up in the Prompts tab as suggestions. Accept one and Notra adds it to your AI scan schedule.

## From rankings to prompts in one click

The Integrations page now has a Google Search Console card next to GitHub and Linear. Connect via OAuth, select the property that matches your website and the weekly sync starts. The property picker opens as soon as OAuth finishes.

Suggestions appear in a new tab on the Prompts page, where you can track a keyword, dismiss it or accept a batch at once. Tracked prompts run in your next scan across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and any other engines you have configured.

## Ranked by where the gap is biggest

Notra ranks suggestions by content opportunity. Queries where you rank well on Google but get few AI mentions come first. Search Console keywords also appear highlighted inside existing prompts, so you can see which terms are already covered.

## Where Google and AI diverge

The integration feeds Content Gaps too. Notra compares your Search Console queries against AI scan results and flags queries where you rank on Google but rarely appear in AI answers, with a create or update action attached to each one.

Go to Integrations, connect Google Search Console and pick your property. The first sync runs right after.
