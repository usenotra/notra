## From rankings to prompts in one click

In Integrations, there's now a dedicated Google Search Console card alongside GitHub and Linear. Connect via OAuth, select the property that matches your website, and the weekly sync starts. The property picker opens right after OAuth so you're never left on a blank screen.

Suggestions land on the Prompts page under a new tab. Track a keyword, dismiss it, or bulk-accept a batch. Tracked prompts run in your next scan across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and any other engines you have configured.

## Ranked by where the gap is biggest

Notra ranks suggestions by content opportunity: queries where your Google coverage is solid but AI mentions are low come first. Search Console keywords also appear highlighted inside existing prompts, so you can see which terms are already covered.

## Where Google and AI diverge

The integration feeds Content Gaps too. Notra compares your Search Console queries against AI scan results and flags queries where you rank on Google but rarely appear in AI answers, with a create or update action attached to each one.
