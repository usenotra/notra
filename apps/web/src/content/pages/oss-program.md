# Notra for Open Source

Notra is free for open source builders. Get the Growth plan at no cost in exchange for honest feedback.

## What you get

Accepted projects use Notra free. The only ask is that you tell us what works and what doesn't.

### Free Notra Growth plan
Full access to the $250/mo Growth plan for as long as you're in the program. No credit card needed.

### Content from your shipped work
Turn commits, PRs and releases into changelogs, launch posts and social updates.

### Marketing assets in your voice
Generate launch visuals and copy in your project's voice.

### A direct line to the team
Shape the roadmap with your feedback.

## Who's eligible

- Your project is publicly available on GitHub.
- It's licensed under an [OSI-approved open source license](https://opensource.org/licenses).
- You're building something useful that benefits from content and marketing.
- You're an owner or maintainer of the repository.
- The project shows active development and community engagement.
