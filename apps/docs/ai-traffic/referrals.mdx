An AI referral is a person who clicked a link in an AI answer and landed on your site. Notra recognizes them by referrer host: chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com, claude.ai, copilot.microsoft.com, you.com, chat.deepseek.com, chat.mistral.ai, grok.com, chat.qwen.ai and Meta AI.

Add **Conversion paths** in [Brand and domains](/geo/brand-and-domains) (for example `/signup` or `/checkout`) to count referrals that reached them.
