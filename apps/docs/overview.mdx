Notra is a Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) platform. It asks AI answer engines such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity the questions your buyers ask, checks whether your brand comes up, and shows you who gets recommended instead. Then it helps you fix the gaps: content gaps, a writer for briefs and articles, and a Studio that turns your GitHub and Linear activity into changelogs, blog posts and social posts.

## What is Notra?

Notra has two sides. The sidebar switches between them.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="GEO" icon="robot">
    Track how AI engines answer prompts about your category, measure share of voice against competitors, watch AI crawlers and referrals hit your site, score how ready your website is for AI agents, and find the questions you are missing from.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Studio" icon="pen-fancy">
    Generate changelogs, blog posts, social posts and images from GitHub, Linear and meeting notes, in your brand voice, on a schedule or when something ships.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Every workspace lives at `app.usenotra.com/{your-slug}`. GEO tracking is scoped by **projects**, so one workspace can track several brands.

## Get started

Onboarding takes four steps and ends with your first scan.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your workspace">
    Pick a name and slug, and add your website. Notra reads the site to pick up your brand voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See what AI says about you">
    Notra reads your website, suggests your brand name and aliases, and pulls a starter set of prompts your buyers might ask.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your competitors">
    Choose the brands you want to be measured against. Notra suggests companies in your field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan and run your first scan">
    Pick a plan, then run a scan from the GEO Overview to see how each engine answers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## GEO

The GEO sidebar has two groups: **Visibility** and **Improve**.

### Visibility

  <Card title="Overview" icon="chart-line" href="/geo/overview">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Prompts" icon="message" href="/geo/prompts-and-competitors">
    The questions Notra asks AI engines on your behalf. Add prompts, import a CSV, or accept suggestions from your Google Search Console queries.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Competitors" icon="users" href="/geo/prompts-and-competitors">

## Studio

Studio is the content side. Its sidebar has Home, Chat (Beta), Content, Analytics and Brand Identity, an **Automation** group with Iris, Schedules and Events, and a **Utility** group with Integrations, Skills and API Keys.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="GitHub" icon="github" href="/integrations/github">

  <Card title="Linear" icon="list-check" href="/integrations/linear">
    Sync issues and updates from Linear for issue context.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Slack" icon="slack" href="/integrations/slack">

### Brand identity

Notra writes in your voice. A workspace can hold several brand identities (for example Default, Marketing, Technical), and you choose one when generating content.

<Info>
  Each identity stores a **company name**, **website**, **description**, **tone** (Conversational, Professional, Casual or Formal, plus custom tone notes), **language**, **custom instructions**, and a description of **who you are writing for**.
</Info>

## What you can build

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Weekly AI visibility check" icon="calendar">
    Set the scan schedule to **Every week** in GEO Settings, keep 10 to 20 prompts and your top competitors tracked, and review share of voice on the Overview each Monday. Content Gaps shows which questions to write for next.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Fill a content gap" icon="pen">
    Open **Content Gaps**, pick a question where competitors are mentioned and you are not, and click **Write**. Approve the brief, wait for the draft to land in Content, edit it, and publish.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Instant release notes" icon="rocket">
    Create an **event-based trigger** for GitHub release events with the Changelog output type. Every published release becomes a draft changelog in your brand voice.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Weekly engineering update" icon="chart-simple">
    Create a **schedule** that runs weekly with a last 7 days lookback across your repositories and the Blog post output type. Turn on auto-publish once you trust the output.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
