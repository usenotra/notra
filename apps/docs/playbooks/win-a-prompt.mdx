Pick one prompt from [Content gaps](/geo/content-gaps) with a high opportunity score. Work it end to end before starting the next.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the answers">
    Open the prompt on the [Prompts](/geo/prompts) page. For each engine, note which brands it recommends, in what order and why. The reasons engines give are your content brief.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the citations">
    Grounded engines save the pages they cited. The engine built its answer from these sources. See [Citations](/geo/metrics/citations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get on the shelf">
    Third-party pages in those citations show up in [Shelf space](/geo/shelf-space). Open a ticket for each page that lists competitors but not you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your own answer">
    Use the [GEO writer](/geo/writer) to draft a page that answers the prompt directly. Choose the format engines already favor for it: a comparison, a listicle or a guide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it readable for agents">
    Run [Agent readiness](/geo/agent-readiness) and check the [AI traffic](/ai-traffic/overview) page to confirm crawlers and assistants fetch the new page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Measure">
    Watch **What changed** over the next scans for **Gained mention** and **Citation gained** on this prompt.
  </Step>
</Steps>
