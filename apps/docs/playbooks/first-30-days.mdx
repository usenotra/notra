This is the order we recommend for a new project. Each week builds on data from the one before, so don't skip ahead to writing content.

## Week 1: Make the data trustworthy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check how engines name you">
    Open a few answers on the [Prompts](/geo/prompts) page and look for product names, abbreviations and your bare domain. Add every variant as an [alias](/geo/brand-and-domains). Missing aliases are the most common reason a mention rate looks too low.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the competitor list">
    Share of voice and content gaps only see [tracked competitors](/geo/competitors). Import the full list by CSV and add synonyms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tune prompts">
    Pause prompts that name your brand or are too broad to ever mention you. Add the questions your sales team hears. See [Writing good prompts](/geo/prompts#writing-good-prompts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick engines and languages">
    Match [engines](/geo/engines) and [languages](/geo/languages) to where your buyers are. Every engine and language multiplies the answers a scan uses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the tracker">
    Add `@usenotra/geo` to your site so you can see AI crawlers and referrals from day one. See [AI traffic](/ai-traffic/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Week 2: Find where you lose

- Read [Content gaps](/geo/content-gaps), sorted by opportunity.
- Open [Shelf space](/geo/shelf-space) and note the third-party pages where competitors are listed and you are not.
- Run [Agent readiness](/geo/agent-readiness) and fix the **Must do** items.

Pick five targets: a mix of prompts to answer with your own content and pages to get listed on.

## Week 3: Ship

- Plan briefs for your top gaps with the [GEO writer](/geo/writer) and publish the drafts.
- Open shelf tickets, assign them and set due dates.
- Make sure new pages are served to AI agents. Check the [AI traffic](/ai-traffic/overview) page for crawler visits on them.

## Week 4: Measure

- Use [What changed](/geo/troubleshooting/numbers-changed#what-changed) on the Overview page to see which prompts moved.
- Compare share of voice with week 1.
- Repeat with the next five targets.

<Tip>
  Engines update their indexes on their own schedule. A new page can take weeks to show up in answers, so judge content by crawler visits first and mentions second.
</Tip>
