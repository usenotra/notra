Shoppers ask AI engines for product recommendations ("best running shoes for flat feet under 150") and Google shows AI Overviews above search results. Across five e-commerce verticals, [Reddit and YouTube appeared in AI citations in every one](https://aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/ecommerce-ai-search-citations-optimization), next to brand product pages, marketplaces and review media.

## Set up

- **Engines.** Commerce brands get Google AI Overview by default. Keep it on next to the chat engines.
- **Prompts.** Track product-level questions with real constraints, such as budget, use case, size or location.
- **Competitors.** Track brands, not retailers. Add product line names as synonyms.
- **Languages.** Add every market you sell in. See [Languages](/geo/languages).

## What to work on

- **Shelf space** usually has the most impact. Review sites, "best of" lists, Reddit threads and YouTube reviews shape most product recommendations. Work them in [Shelf space](/geo/shelf-space).
- **Product and category pages** should state the facts engines compare: price, materials, sizes and ratings. Make sure assistants can fetch them. Check **Cited in answer** on the [AI traffic](/ai-traffic/overview) page.
- **Buying guides.** Write them with the [GEO writer](/geo/writer) in the listicle or comparison format.

## Metrics to watch

- Mention rate and position per engine, especially Google AI Overview.
- Performance by language for each market.
- AI referrals reaching checkout. Add `/checkout` as a **Conversion path**.

<Note>
  ChatGPT and Google also show product cards and shopping ads inside AI answers. Notra tracks the answer text and its citations, not those product cards or ads.
</Note>
