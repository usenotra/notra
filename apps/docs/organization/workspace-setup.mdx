## Creating Your First Workspace

When you sign up for Notra you are taken through a four-step onboarding flow. The first step creates your organization, which is the workspace where content, GEO tracking, automations, integrations, and team members live. Every workspace URL starts with its slug: `app.usenotra.com/your-slug`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Workspace">
    Enter an organization name and a slug. The slug is generated from the name and you can edit it. Add your website so Notra can pick up your brand voice from it, and tell us where you heard about Notra.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Visibility">
    Notra asks ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity what your buyers ask them and checks whether your brand comes up. Pick up to 20 prompts to track from the suggestions or add your own.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Competitors">
    Confirm the competitors Notra detected or add the ones you want to track share of voice against.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pricing">
    Choose a plan. GEO tracking requires a Starter, Growth, or Scale plan. If a plan offers a free trial the button reads **Start free trial**. See [Billing & Plans](/organization/billing).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  While the visibility and competitor steps run, Notra also analyzes your website in the background to create a default brand identity. If you already have a workspace with a paid subscription, onboarding is skipped and you land in that workspace.
</Info>

### Creating Additional Workspaces

Click the organization name at the top of the sidebar and choose the option to create a new organization. The dialog asks for a name, a slug, and an optional website. You can belong to as many organizations as you like, with a different role in each.

## Organization Settings

Manage your workspace from **Settings > General**.

### Basic Information

<ParamField path="Name" type="string" required>
  The display name of your organization. Must be between 2 and 100 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Slug" type="string" required>
  A URL-friendly identifier used in workspace URLs. Must be 3 to 63 characters: lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only.

  <Warning>Reserved slugs cannot be used: `api`, `auth`, `login`, `signup`, `forgot-password`, `reset-password`, `invitation`, `callback`, `onboarding`, `dashboard`, `rpc`, `design-system`, `settings`, `admin`, `help`, `support`, `docs`, `blog`, `about`, `terms`, `privacy`, `contact`.</Warning>
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Logo" type="image">
  Click the logo to upload a new one. Accepted formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, AVIF.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="ID" type="string">
  The organization ID. Read-only; use it when a request or support ticket needs to identify the workspace.
</ParamField>

### Updating Workspace Settings

1. Navigate to **Settings > General**
2. Click the logo to upload a new image; it saves immediately
3. Update the name or slug
4. Click **Save Changes**

<Note>
  Changing the slug changes every workspace URL. Notra redirects you to the new slug after saving; update bookmarks and any links you have shared.
</Note>

Only owners and admins can save organization settings.

| **Logs**            | Automation and API activity for the workspace. Retention depends on your plan                     |

## Two-Factor Authentication

Notra supports two-factor authentication with any TOTP authenticator app such as 1Password, Google Authenticator or Authy. Once it is on, every sign-in asks for a code from your app after your password or your Google or GitHub login.

### Turning It On

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account settings">
    Go to **Settings > Account** and find **Two-factor authentication**. Click **Set up**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Scan it with your authenticator app. If you cannot scan it, click **Can't scan it?** to copy the setup key instead.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the code">
    Type the 6-digit code your app shows. Two-factor authentication is on as soon as the code is accepted.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your backup codes">
    Notra shows 10 one-time backup codes. Store them somewhere safe. Each code works once and lets you sign in if you lose access to your authenticator app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Signing In With a Backup Code

On the code prompt during sign-in, choose the backup code option and enter one of your unused codes. Signing in this way removes the authenticator from your account so you can set up a new one from **Settings > Account**.

### Managing Backup Codes

**Settings > Account** shows how many backup codes are left. Click **Regenerate** to get a fresh set. Confirm with a code from your authenticator app or an unused backup code. Regenerating invalidates every earlier code.

### Turning It Off

Under **Two-factor authentication**, click **Remove** next to your authenticator app and confirm with a code from the app or an unused backup code. Your backup codes are deleted at the same time.

<Note>
  Backup codes and a fresh code from your authenticator app are the only ways to turn two-factor authentication off or regenerate codes. Being signed in is not enough on its own.
</Note>

## Brand Identity

Notra writes content in your voice. Brand identities live under **Studio > Brand Identity** in the sidebar. A workspace can have several identities (for example Marketing, Technical, Internal), one of which is the default.

The page has four tabs:

* **Company Info**: profile, tone, language, and audience for the selected identity
* **References**: real posts that teach the writer your style
* **Sitemap**: pages Notra can link to and monitor for AI discovery
* **Brand Guidelines**: logos, colors, typography, and screenshots pulled from your site

### Website Analysis

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your website URL">
    On a new workspace, or when adding an identity, enter your website (for example `example.com`) and click **Analyze**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for the analysis">
    Notra scrapes the site, extracts the profile, and saves it. The stepper shows **Scraping**, **Extracting**, and **Saving**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the result">
    The company name, description, and tone are filled in from your site. Adjust anything that is off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To run it again later, open the identity menu and choose **Re-analyze**.

### Company Info Fields

<ParamField path="Company name" type="string" required>
  Your company or brand name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Website" type="url">
  Your website. Used for analysis and as reference material.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Description" type="string">
  A short overview of your company: what you do and who you serve.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Tone Profile" type="enum">
  One of **Conversational**, **Professional**, **Casual**, or **Formal**. Switch to a custom tone to describe the voice in your own words instead.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Language" type="enum">
  The language generated content is written in.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Custom Instructions" type="string">
  Rules the writer must follow: phrases to avoid, required terminology, formatting preferences.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Who are you writing for?" type="string">
  Your target audience: their interests, pain points, and what matters to them.
</ParamField>

<Info>
  Company Info auto-saves a few seconds after you stop typing. Changes apply to the next piece of content you generate.
</Info>

See [Brand Voice](/concepts/brand-voice) for how each setting affects the output.

### Managing Identities

From the identity selector you can add an identity (name and website), set one as the default, re-analyze it, or delete it. Automations and the create dialog let you pick which identity writes each post.

## Workspace Management

### Switching Between Workspaces

Click the organization name at the top of the sidebar and pick another organization. Notra remembers the workspace you visited last and opens it on your next sign-in.

You can also switch from **Settings > Account**, where every organization you belong to is listed with a **View** button.

### Leaving a Workspace

In **Settings > Account**, use **Leave** next to an organization you do not own. Owners cannot leave; they delete the organization instead. You must always keep at least one organization.

### Deleting a Workspace

<Warning>
  Deleting a workspace permanently removes all of its data: posts, GEO tracking, automations, integrations, uploaded files, and memberships. The subscription is cancelled. This cannot be undone.
</Warning>

To delete a workspace:

1. Navigate to **Settings > General**
2. Scroll to **Danger Zone**
3. Click **Delete Organization**
4. Confirm

**Requirements:**

* You must be the workspace owner
* You must belong to at least one other organization
* If other members exist, you are warned before the deletion goes through

## Best Practices

<Tip>
  **Choose a memorable slug.** It appears in every workspace URL. Pick something short and recognizable.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Add references.** A few real posts on the References tab do more for output quality than any amount of tone description.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Keep the brand identity current.** Re-analyze after a site redesign or a change in positioning, and review the auto-filled fields.
</Tip>
