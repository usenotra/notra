## Overview

Every organization has one owner and any number of admins and members. Owners and admins manage the organization and its people, while members do the work. Notra sends invitations by email, and you manage them from **Settings > Members**.

## Inviting team members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Members page">
    Go to **Settings > Members**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite">
    Click **Invite Member** and enter the person's email address. Notra rejects disposable email addresses.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a role">
    Pick **Member** or **Admin**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send">
    Click send, and the recipient gets an email saying you invited them to join the organization, with a link to accept.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Only owners and admins can invite people.
</Info>

### Team member limits

Each plan includes a number of team member seats. When the organization has used them all, sending or resending an invitation fails with a message and a **View plans** link to **Settings > Billing & Usage**, where you can upgrade to add more seats.

## Roles and permissions

Notra has three roles, and the server enforces permissions, so the rules below apply to the dashboard and to anything that acts on your behalf.

### Owner

The owner is the person who created the organization, and there is exactly one owner.

* Everything an admin can do
* Delete the organization
* Cannot be removed, and cannot have their role changed
* Cannot leave the organization, only delete it

<Warning>
  You cannot transfer ownership from the Members page, because ownership only moves during account deletion. When an owner deletes their Notra account, they choose for each organization they own whether to transfer it to another member or delete it.
</Warning>

### Admin

* Change organization settings (name, slug, logo)
* Invite people, change roles between Member and Admin and remove members
* Resend and cancel invitations
* Everything a member can do

### Member

* Create, edit, publish and delete content
* Use chat, GEO, schedules, event triggers, integrations, brand identities, skills and API keys
* View the members list and pending invitations

Members cannot change organization settings or manage other people.

<Note>
  Billing is open to every role, so anyone in the organization can open **Settings > Billing & Usage**, change the plan and open the customer portal.
</Note>

## Managing team members

The **Members** tab lists every person in the organization with their name, email and role.

### Changing a role

1. Find the member in the list
2. Open the action menu at the end of the row
3. Choose **Change role**
4. Pick **Member** or **Admin** and confirm

Role changes take effect immediately.

### Removing a member

1. Find the member in the list
2. Open the action menu
3. Choose the remove option and confirm

<Warning>
  Removal takes effect immediately, and the person loses access to the organization and everything in it.
</Warning>

The owner's row has no action menu because the owner cannot be changed or removed.

## Pending invitations

The **Pending** tab shows the number of open invitations and lists each one with the email address, the role it grants and when it expires.

**Resend an invitation**

1. Open the action menu on the invitation
2. Choose resend and confirm
3. Notra sends a fresh email

**Cancel an invitation**

1. Open the action menu on the invitation
2. Choose cancel and confirm
3. The link in the email stops working

<Note>
  Invitations expire, so if someone has not accepted by the date in the Expires column, resend it.
</Note>

## Accepting an invitation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the email">
    Click the link in the invitation email.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in or create an account">
    If you do not have a Notra account, create one with the invited email address. If you already have one, sign in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start working">
    Notra adds you to the organization with the role from the invitation and opens the workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Multiple organizations

You can belong to several organizations with a different role in each.

### Switching organizations

Click the organization name at the top of the sidebar and pick another one, and Notra remembers the workspace you visited last.

### Managing your memberships

Go to **Settings > Account** and scroll to the organizations section, which lists every organization you belong to with two actions:

* **View**: switch to that organization
* **Leave**: remove yourself from an organization you do not own

Owners see a delete option instead of leave, and you must always keep at least one organization.

<Warning>
  If you leave an organization, you need a new invitation to get back in.
</Warning>

## Best practices

<Tip>
  Use Member by default and give Admin only to people who need to change settings or manage the team.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  Review the list regularly so you can remove people who no longer need access and cancel invitations that were never accepted.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  Invite the right email address, because each invitation is tied to the address you enter and the person should sign in with that address.
</Tip>
