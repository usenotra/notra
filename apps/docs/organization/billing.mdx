## Overview

Billing is per organization. Open **Settings > Billing & Usage** to change your plan, add the zero data retention add-on, see invoices and track usage. Manage AI credits under **Settings > Credits**.

## Plans

Notra has three self-serve plans plus Enterprise. Every plan is available monthly or annually.

| Plan        | Monthly | Annual  | AI answers tracked / mo | Long-form posts / mo | Image generations / mo | Projects | References |
| ----------- | ------- | ------- | ----------------------- | -------------------- | ---------------------- | -------- | ---------- |
| **Starter** | $100    | $1,000  | 2,000                   | 10                   | 8                      | 1        | 100        |
| **Growth**  | $250    | $2,500  | 6,000                   | 25                   | 20                     | 3        | 500        |
| **Scale**   | $550    | $5,500  | 12,000                  | 50                   | 45                     | 10       | 1,000      |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Custom | Unlimited             | Unlimited            | Unlimited              | Unlimited | Unlimited |

All plans include unlimited social posts and unlimited tracked prompts across all major AI models.

References beyond the included amount cost, per reference per month, $0.05 on Starter, $0.04 on Growth and $0.03 on Scale.

Starter and Growth get standard support plus Slack. Scale gets priority support and Enterprise gets dedicated support.

Zero data retention is a paid add-on on Starter, Growth and Scale, and is included on Enterprise.

<Info>
  The plan cards on the billing page come from the live billing catalog. If they ever differ from this table, the plan cards are correct.
</Info>

### What the quotas mean

* **AI answers tracked.** Every answer Notra collects from an AI model during a GEO scan counts as one. Persona conversations count twice per answer. When a workspace pays with AI credits instead, persona runs cost twice their model usage. This quota covers prompt tracking, competitor tracking and share of voice.
* **Long-form posts.** Changelogs and blog posts.
* **Social posts.** LinkedIn and X posts. Unlimited on every plan.
* **Image generations.** Generated social images.
* **Projects.** The number of brands or products you can track separately in GEO.
* **References.** Real posts attached to brand identities to teach the writer your voice.

Quotas reset monthly.

### Enterprise

For custom limits, included zero data retention and dedicated support, contact [hello@usenotra.com](mailto:hello@usenotra.com).

### Legacy Basic and Pro plans

Organizations that subscribed before the current plans launched can stay on Basic or Pro. The billing page shows them as the equivalent Starter or Growth tier, and invoices still say Basic or Pro. Switching to a current plan is a normal plan change.

### Without a plan

An organization without an active plan can sign in and manage settings, but:

* GEO pages are locked, because GEO requires a Starter, Growth or Scale plan
* API requests return `402 Active subscription required`
* Content generation needs plan quota or AI credits

## Managing your subscription

### Choosing a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Go to **Settings > Billing & Usage**. The **Billing** tab is selected by default.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick an interval">
    Toggle between **Monthly** and **Yearly** above the plan cards.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Optionally add zero data retention">
    Each card has a toggle to include the ZDR add-on in the same checkout.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the plan">
    Click the button on the card. It reads **Get Started**, **Upgrade** or **Downgrade** depending on your current plan. It reads **Start Free Trial** when the plan offers a trial you are eligible for.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete checkout">
    Notra sends you to a secure checkout page for payment details, then back to Notra. Your new limits apply as soon as the subscription is active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your active plan has a **Current** badge, or **Trial** while a trial is running.

### Changing or cancelling

Click **Manage Subscription** at the top of the billing page to open the customer portal. There you can update the payment method, download invoices and cancel. You can also pick a different plan card to move between tiers.

<Note>
  Scheduled changes, such as a downgrade or cancellation that takes effect at the end of the current period, show on the plan card as **Plan Scheduled**.
</Note>

## Zero data retention add-on

Zero data retention (ZDR) routes your prompts and answers only through model hosts that keep no prompt or answer data. It costs 20% of your plan price and is available on Starter, Growth and Scale.

Enable it from the **Add-ons** section of the billing page, or with the toggle on a plan card during checkout. Before it activates, you must acknowledge the following:

* Availability is not guaranteed for every model. ZDR exists only where the gateway Notra routes through holds a zero-data-retention agreement with that model's provider.
* New models usually launch without ZDR. Notra excludes them until a ZDR option exists.
* Notra skips models without a ZDR host. GEO settings marks them, and they do not run unless you approve each one.
* Gateway and provider terms can change. If a provider withdraws an agreement, Notra excludes that model from your scans until an alternative is available.

Removing the add-on takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle.

## AI credits

AI credits are a prepaid dollar balance that Notra draws on when a plan quota runs out. When a long-form post, image generation or GEO scan would exceed your monthly quota, Notra charges credits instead. If there are no credits either, Notra blocks the action and asks you to upgrade or add credits.

### Viewing and topping up

**Settings > Credits** shows your remaining balance, how much of the included balance you have used and a usage chart. It only appears when your plan includes AI credits.

Click **Top Up Credits** to add between $5 and $500 in one purchase. The dialog offers $5, $10, $25 and $50 presets or a custom amount, then takes you through checkout.

### Automatic pauses

Notra pauses automations that fail because credits ran out or a plan limit was reached, and emails owners the reason. Add credits or upgrade, then re-enable the schedule or event trigger.

## Invoices

The **Invoices** table on the billing page lists every invoice with:

* **Date**. Click the column header to sort ascending or descending.
* **Description**. The plan or add-on charged, for example Starter, Growth, Zero data retention or AI Credits Top-up.
* **Amount**
* **Status**

Click a row to open the hosted invoice, where you can download a PDF and see the itemized charges.

## Usage tracking

Open the **Usage** tab on the billing page to see, for the current period:

* Every limited feature with the amount used, the limit and a progress bar
* Features that are unlimited on your plan
* AI credits spent

<Warning>
  When a metered quota is used up, the affected feature falls back to AI credits. With no credits available, the feature stays blocked until the quota resets or you upgrade.
</Warning>

Team member seats are also plan-limited. When the organization is out of seats, invitations fail with a **View plans** link. See [team management](/organization/team-management).

## Billing FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Some plans offer a trial. If one is available to you, the plan card button reads **Start Free Trial** and the card shows a **Trial** badge while it runs. If you see **Get Started** instead, that plan has no trial for your organization.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel anytime?">
    Yes. Open **Manage Subscription** and cancel from the customer portal. Your plan stays active until the end of the current period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do quotas roll over?">
    No. Monthly quotas reset at the start of each period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I upgrade mid-cycle?">
    Yes. Pick the new plan on the billing page and complete checkout. The new limits apply immediately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who can change billing?">
    Anyone in the organization can open the billing page and change the plan. Restrict membership if you want to limit this.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you offer refunds?">
    We handle refunds case by case. Contact [hello@usenotra.com](mailto:hello@usenotra.com) with the invoice details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Best practices

<Tip>
  **Pick annual billing if you are staying.** Annual plans are priced at ten times the monthly price.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Watch AI answers.** GEO scans are the quota most teams hit first. Reduce the number of tracked prompts or models, or move up a tier, before the month runs out.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Keep a small credit balance.** A top-up prevents automations from pausing when a quota runs out near the end of the period.
</Tip>
