**Analytics** in Studio reports on the X and LinkedIn accounts you connect from the Analytics page.

## Metrics

| Network | What Notra shows |
| --- | --- |
| X | Followers, following, posts and listed counts. Per post: impressions, likes, replies, reposts, quotes and bookmarks, plus engagement rate |
| LinkedIn | Profile details only, since Notra doesn't sync post metrics yet |

Notra skips replies and reposts when it reads your X posts, and it syncs data twice a day at 06:00 and 18:00 UTC.

## Leaderboard

The **Leaderboard** ranks X accounts side by side, for example your founders or competitors.

- **Track an account** by entering its X handle, and Notra syncs it right away.
- **Stop tracking** removes an account and deletes its synced data, and you can stop tracking several accounts at once.

You can only add X accounts to the leaderboard.
