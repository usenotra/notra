## How it is calculated

**Mention rate = answers that mention you / all saved answers**, for the selected engines, languages and date range.

- An answer counts as a mention when the judge finds your company name or any [alias](/geo/brand-and-domains) in it.
- Every saved answer has equal weight and answers that do not name you count as zero, so adding prompts you rank poorly on lowers the rate.
- The dashboard shows the delta against the first half of the selected range.
- The rate counts every saved answer in the range: all tracked languages, [conversations](/geo/conversations) and [persona](/geo/personas) conversations. A conversation counts as one answer, and it counts as a mention when any of its turns mentions you.
- Per-prompt views show each tracked prompt once, in your prompt language.

## Where to find it

- **Mentions** card and **Mention rate by engine** table on the GEO **Overview** page.

## What moves it

- Prompt mix: adding or pausing [prompts](/geo/prompts) changes the denominator.
- Engine mix: each [engine](/geo/engines) has its own rate, so adding a weak engine pulls the total down.
- Aliases: a missing product name or domain alias hides real mentions.
- Content: closing [content gaps](/geo/content-gaps) and earning [shelf space](/geo/shelf-space) gets more answers to name you, while the other three only change what Notra counts rather than how often engines mention you.
