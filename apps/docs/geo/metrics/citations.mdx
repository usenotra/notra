Engines with web search look up pages before they answer. Notra saves the search queries they ran and the sources they cited for every answer.

A mention means the answer names your brand, while a citation means the engine linked a page as a source. You can be mentioned without being cited, and an engine can cite your page without recommending you.

## Which engines return citations

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overview all return their sources, and so do OpenCode, Claude Code and Codex when they are enabled. Engines labelled **No web search**, such as Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral and Cursor, answer without searching, so their answers never have citations.

## Your own pages

Notra marks an answer as citing you when any source URL is on your brand website or one of your [tracked domains](/geo/brand-and-domains). The Overview page counts these citations per engine, but there is no separate citation rate metric yet.

## Where to find them

- Answer details on the [Prompts](/geo/prompts) page list the cited sources and search queries.
- [Shelf Space](/geo/shelf-space) groups cited third-party pages and tracks whether you are listed on them.
- The **What changed** card flags **Citation gained**, **Citation lost** and **Competitor cited**.
