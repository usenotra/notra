Grounded engines search the web before they answer. Notra saves the search queries they ran and the sources they cited for every answer.

## Where to find them

- Answer details on the [Prompts](/geo/prompts) page list the cited sources and search queries.
- [Shelf Space](/geo/shelf-space) groups cited third-party pages and tracks whether you are listed on them.
- The **What changed** card flags **Citation gained**, **Citation lost** and **Competitor cited**.
- `sources[]` and `searchQueries` in `GET /v1/projects/{projectId}/geo/visibility/prompt-results`.
