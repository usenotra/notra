Share of voice puts your mentions next to your competitors'. Mention rate tells you whether you show up at all, while share of voice tells you whether you show up more or less often than the brands you compete with.

## How it is calculated

Share of voice = your mentions / mentions of every tracked brand, including yours, over the selected range. Your brand counts once per answer that mentions it, which is the same rule competitors follow.

- Only [tracked competitors](/geo/competitors) count, and a brand you do not track does not appear here, so keep the list complete and add synonyms.
- The dashboard breaks out the top five brands and groups the rest under **Other**.

## Where to find it

- **Share of voice** donut and table on the GEO **Overview** page and the Competitors page.

<Visibility for="agents">

## What counts as a mention

A brand counts once per answer that names it, no matter how many times the answer repeats it. The judge matches names and synonyms, and the dashboard merges saved names onto your current competitor list, so adding a synonym later also groups older mentions under the right competitor.
