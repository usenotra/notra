The dashboard sidebar has two modes. **GEO** is the default and the focus of these docs, while **Studio** is the content side. Every GEO page reads the active [project](/geo/projects) from the sidebar.

## Visibility

| Page | What it shows | Docs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overview | Mention rate, share of voice, What changed, sentiment and agent journeys | [Mention rate](/geo/metrics/mention-rate) |
| Traffic | AI crawlers, AI-cited fetches and AI referrals on your site | [AI traffic](/ai-traffic/overview) |
| Prompts | The questions Notra asks every engine, plus conversations and Search Console suggestions | [Prompts](/geo/prompts) |
| Competitors | Who engines recommend instead of you | [Competitors](/geo/competitors) |
| Personas | Buyer personas that research your category in each engine with web search (beta) | [Personas](/geo/personas) |

## Improve

| Page | What it shows | Docs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content Gaps | Questions engines answer without mentioning you | [Content gaps](/geo/content-gaps) |
| Shelf Space | Third-party pages engines cite, and whether you are listed on them | [Shelf space](/geo/shelf-space) |
| Agent Readiness | A score for how well agents can discover and use your site | [Agent readiness](/geo/agent-readiness) |
| Write | Briefs and drafts that answer a gap | [GEO writer](/geo/writer) |
| Content and Schedules | Drafts and recurring generation, shared with Studio | [Content](/content/blog-posts) |

GEO settings (brand, languages and models) open from the settings menu, and the pages for them are [Brand and domains](/geo/brand-and-domains), [Engines](/geo/engines) and [Languages](/geo/languages).

## Studio

Studio turns what you ship into changelogs, blog posts and social posts. It has Content, Chat, Brand Identity, [Social analytics](/studio/social-analytics), [Automation](/automation/overview) and [Integrations](/integrations/overview), plus the agent [Feedback](/feedback/inbox) inbox.
