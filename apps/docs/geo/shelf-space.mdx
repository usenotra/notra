When engines recommend products, they rely on a small set of pages: review sites, "best tools" listicles, community threads and news. If those pages list competitors and not you, engines recommend the competitors. Shelf Space finds those pages and lets your team open a ticket for each one.

## What a shelf is

A shelf is a third-party page that engines cited while answering your tracked prompts. Each shelf shows:

- The page and its kind, for example **Review site**, **Community** or **News**.
- How many answers cited it, which engines cited it and when it was first and last cited.
- **Who is on the shelf**: your brand and each tracked competitor, marked **On shelf** or **Missing**.

Placement is either **Verified by fetching the page** or **Marked by a teammate**. When Notra has not checked a page yet, open the row to mark it.

## Work a shelf

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find an opportunity">
    Notra flags pages that list competitors but not you as opportunities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a ticket">
    **Open ticket** assigns the page to a teammate with an optional due date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get listed">
    Pitch the editor, submit a review or join the thread. Set the ticket status to open, in progress, won, lost or dismissed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Once your brand is on the page, mark it **On shelf**. Later scans show whether engines start mentioning you for the prompts that cite it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Filter the board by tickets assigned to you, unassigned tickets or closed tickets. **Add shelf** tracks a page engines have not cited yet but you want to be on.
